Running a learning program means managing a lot of moving parts. Content rollouts, learner access, reporting, mobile experience. And for a long time, too many of those parts required manual workarounds: duplicating courses to work on content before it's ready, pulling fragmented data, and building reports that never quite reflected how your program actually ran.
